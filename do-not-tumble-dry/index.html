Do Not Tumble Dry

An iPhone game by Boris Timofeenko. You hold the phone upright and turn it like a wheel; the game counts full turns.

Support

Write to timofeenkoboris@gmail.com. Say which iPhone and iOS version you have and what happened.

Privacy policy

The game collects no data. It has no ads, no analytics and no accounts, and it sends nothing to me.

Your results are stored on your phone. If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements go to Apple's Game Center, under Apple's privacy policy.

The picture of a result leaves the phone only when you share or save it yourself.
