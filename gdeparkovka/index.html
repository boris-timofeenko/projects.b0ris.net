GdeParkovka

“Where’s parking”: a map of free parking in Russia, where drivers add the points.

Address
gdeparkovka.ru, in Russian
Live since
6 July 2026
Points
367 on the public map
Written in
Node.js and Express, SQLite, plain JavaScript with Leaflet and OpenStreetMap

In Saint Petersburg I didn’t want to pay for parking, so when I drove past an empty courtyard or a pocket where a car bothers no one, I saved it in my navigator. The list went around among friends, and I decided it should be a public map.

I built it with Claude Code in one day, after gdebenz.ru, a map of fuel stations. The first points were my own list, imported from Yandex Maps. Anyone can add a point without registering, and new points go to moderation. Now there are 367.

I left the design as the model made it, emoji included, on purpose. The point is that the map works and gives useful information. I asked Claude to compress photos fairly aggressively, because a driver only needs to see the general view of the parking lot; they now go out as JPEG quality 45, at most 1024 px. I also asked for a progress bar while a photo uploads, “preferably a real one, not a lying one”.

Some residents of the city centre downvoted points next to their houses to keep drivers away, though I had seen those spots myself. Comments got spam and swearing, so now they are checked against a dictionary of word roots that sees through Latin look-alike letters and spaced-out words. Anyone could change a point’s capacity, so capacity became a vote, and the map shows the option most people chose.

It runs as one Node.js process with SQLite, tuned for 1 CPU and 512 MB. I moved it from an expensive host to a cheap one with a hard disk.
